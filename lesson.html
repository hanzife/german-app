Personalpronomen

German pronouns are a must-know! They are like tiny words that stand in for nouns, helping you avoid repetition. In this lesson, we'll focus on the personal pronouns in the nominative case.

Beginner
5 min

Singular Pronouns

These are used for one person or thing. Note the formal and informal 'you'

  • Ich I
  • Du You (informal)
  • Er He
  • Sie She
  • Es It

Plural Pronouns

These are used for more than one person or thing. The formal 'you' is also used for a group

  • Wir We
  • Ihr You
  • Sie They (plural, informal)
  • Sie You (formal, singular & plural)
